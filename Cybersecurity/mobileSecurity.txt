Mobile Device Security

Management policies like MDM (Mobile Device Management), MAM (Mobile Application Management), and MIM (Mobile 
Identity Management) exist to allow organizations to control access and security on the mobile devices of 
their employees. These policies are often combined with ownership control polices (BYOD, CYOD, COBO, and 
COPE) in EMM (Enterprise Mobile Management) systems.  

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ 

Management Policy:

Mobile Device Management (MDM) - allows the organization to control security features like data access, 
password policy, data usage, app access, etc. on mobile devices in their network

Mobile Application Management (MAM) - allows many of the same security features as MDM but on the application 
level, allowing organizations to control security throughout an application life cycle

Mobile Identity Management (MIM) - an authentication method that uses a device's SIM card as an identifier, 
this allows the organization to identify and authorize deveces which enables access control models

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ownership Policy:

Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) - the employee brings their own device
- Pros - less device cost, emplyees use a device they are comfortable with
- Cons - security/data risk, supporting all devices is difficult

Choose Your Own Device (CYOD) - employees choose a device from a list of alloted devices
- Pros - less security risk, less need for widespread support
- Cons - associated cost

Corporate Owned Business Only (COBO) - employees are restricted to strict use of company owned devices that 
are used for business only
- Pros - far less security risk, all employees have identical devices which gets rid of the need for 
supporting of multiple devices
- Cons - high cost, employees have 2 devices

Corporate Owned Personally Enabled (COPE) - employees use company issued devices but they are given ability 
to use it for personal activity
- Pros - reduced security risk
- Cons - some risk remains because it is hard to control data outside of the organization, employee privacy 
risk, high cost

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Mobile Device Connection Methods:

Wireless Fidelity (Wi-Fi) - essentially identical to Wi-Fi connection on PC, just on mobile

Mobile Network/Cellular - a network of cell towers form a grid that allows mobile devices to communicate with 
eachother, towers act as internet routers

Bluetooth - short-range, local, wireless communication using radio waves

Near Field Communication (NFC) - short-range wireless communication using electromagnetic induction

Mobile Hotspot - uses cellular technology to create a 'mobile Wi-Fi router' allowing other devices to connect 
and use it like Wi-Fi

Infrared - uses IR light to transmit electrical signals
